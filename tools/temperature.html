Convert a temperature

Converted values are rounded to 4 decimal places. Water reference points assume standard atmospheric pressure.

70 °F = 21.1111 °C

Conversion formulas

°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9
°F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32

At −40°, Fahrenheit and Celsius are equal.
