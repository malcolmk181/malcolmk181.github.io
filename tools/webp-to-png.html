Make a PNG ready for your next Slack emoji. Images stay on your device.

Drop an image here, choose a file, or paste with Ctrl+V / ⌘V.

One image at a time. WebP, PNG, JPEG, and GIF supported. Animated images become a still PNG of the first frame.

Transparency preserves transparent pixels and padding; it does not remove an existing background. Small images are enlarged for square output.

Output starts at 1024 × 1024. Slack recommends square images under 128 KB, ideally with transparency; choose a smaller preset if needed.
